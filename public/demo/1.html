06 / What makes an agent?

Model + Instructions + Knowledge + Tools

MODEL

Generate & reason

Interprets requests and produces responses.

INSTRUCTIONS

Set behavior

Define role, boundaries, and response style.

KNOWLEDGE

Ground answers

Retrieve relevant information from sources.

TOOLS

Take action

Call functions, APIs, or other capabilities.

GOAL

Accomplish a task

Coordinate capabilities around the user’s objective.

GUARDRAILS

Stay controlled

Limit unsafe, irrelevant, or unsupported behavior.

Each ingredient maps to something students will click in the Azure portal next.

10 / Responsible AI

Capability needs boundaries

Grounding

Prefer relevant sources over invented facts.

Permissions

Give tools only the access they need.

Human oversight

Keep people involved in consequential actions.

Privacy

Don’t casually feed sensitive personal data into demos.

Keep this practical. Connect each principle to the agent students just designed.
